## Domain

`grant-access` *(closest fit — the problem is discovery/access of funding, for startups rather than charities)*

## The problem

Small, early-stage New Zealand startups struggle to find angel and seed funding — not
only because capital is scarce, but because **visibility and access are broken**:

- **It's who you know.** Angel networks, chambers of commerce and investor clubs
  operate as closed circles. Even people *inside* them often don't know how to open
  the doors to connect actual investors with actual founders. Founders without warm
  networks — first-timers, founders outside Auckland/Wellington, Māori and Pasifika
  founders, women — are structurally locked out.
- **The visible funnel is brutally narrow.** The high-profile pathways take in
  hundreds of applicants and fund a handful (founder-observed: on the order of 400
  startups through the door for ~6 funded). Everyone else exits with nothing — no
  routing to the funders who *would* fit them.
- **The smallest cheques have no marketplace.** The $10k–$50k and $100k–$200k
  angel cheques that get a startup moving exist, but there is nowhere they are
  surfaced. Most visible infrastructure (funds, NZGCP Aspire co-investment) starts
  around $250k–$1m — below that mark, founders are on their own.
- **Access is pay-to-play.** The conferences, cohorts and accelerator programmes
  where investor connections actually happen cost money or are competitive-entry —
  and the founders who most need those connections are the ones who can least afford
  them or don't get selected.
- **The dominant event format doesn't connect people.** Pitch nights showcase a
  "top 5" while every other founder sits in the audience. There is no NZ room —
  physical or digital — where *all* the founders and *all* the investors actually
  talk to each other.

There is no single NZ-focused place where a founder can see who invests at their
stage, in their region, at their cheque size, and how to approach them. The
funding-discovery tools founders find online (Metal, Cherub, most grant marketplaces)
are built for the US or other markets, not Aotearoa.

## Why it matters (any evidence you have)

- 2025 was a **record year** for NZ startup investment ($754m, up 61%), yet only
  **47 genuinely new companies received investment** — below the historical norm of
  50+ — and 83% of capital went to expansion-stage companies. Only 21% of H1 2025
  deals went to genuinely new companies vs a ~35% historical average.
  ([Young Company Finance Autumn 2026, via b2bnews](https://b2bnews.co.nz/news/nz-startup-investment-hits-754m-but-new-company-pipeline-shrinks/);
  [NZ Herald](https://www.nzherald.co.nz/business/personal-finance/investment/start-up-company-investment-surged-to-754m-in-2025-after-deal-activity-jumped/5B4X67OA4FANLGYZIUCXHTDLGI/))
- Angel capital was nearly **flat in 2025** (+2.7% to $13.9m) while deal count jumped
  34% — the same money spread much thinner per company. Average investment per angel
  fell to ~$12,400 — exactly the micro-cheque segment with no marketplace.
  ([Catalist NZ Angel Market Report, via b2bnews](https://b2bnews.co.nz/news/nz-startup-investment-hits-754m-but-new-company-pipeline-shrinks/))
- MBIE's May 2025 briefing on access to growth capital acknowledges "regression in
  the seed funding market due to recent economic conditions".
  ([MBIE briefing](https://www.mbie.govt.nz/dmsdocument/31135-access-growth-capital))
- The Startup Advisors Council's **Upstart Nation** report (2023) found the ecosystem
  "noisy, fragmented, and disconnected", with government support spread across
  multiple agencies "making it difficult for founders to find support".
  ([Upstart Nation, MBIE](https://www.mbie.govt.nz/assets/upstart-nation.pdf);
  [Simpson Grierson summary](https://www.simpsongrierson.com/insights-news/legal-updates/upstart-nation-building-a-sustainable-startup-ecosystem))
- Discovery is churning right now: Callaghan Innovation was **disestablished on
  1 December 2025** with grants and startup programmes moved to MBIE — founders'
  mental maps of "where funding lives" broke overnight.
  ([Callaghan disestablishment updates](https://www.callaghaninnovation.govt.nz/about-us/disestablishment-updates/))
- **Lived experience** (this issue's author): a founder raising under the $250k mark,
  not selected for the women-founder conference/cohort pathways (e.g. Electrify
  Aotearoa and related programmes), unable to justify paid conference access, and
  with no visible route to the angels writing $10k–$200k cheques. Members of local
  chambers and angel groups I've spoken to can't articulate how a founder gets
  connected either — the doors are opaque even from the inside.

## What's already being done?

- **Angel Association NZ** is the peak body for ~13 angel networks but doesn't offer
  founder-facing, machine-readable discovery ([angelassociation.co.nz](https://www.angelassociation.co.nz/)).
- **Enterprise Angels** (Tauranga) lists deals on its AngelEquity platform — one of
  the few open-ish windows into angel deal flow ([angelequity.co.nz](https://www.angelequity.co.nz/home/about/angel-networks/)).
- **NZ Investment Network** ([newzealandinvestmentnetwork.co.nz](https://www.newzealandinvestmentnetwork.co.nz/))
  is the NZ arm of a global founder-investor matching franchise — existence verified,
  activity level and founder outcomes unverified.
- **NZGCP Aspire** co-invests at seed but only alongside private investors you've
  already found ([Aspire](https://www.nzgcp.co.nz/funding/aspire-seed-fund)) — it
  presumes the access problem is already solved.
- **NZTE** offers capital-raise support and 'InvestEd' education ([NZTE](https://www.nzte.govt.nz/page/invest-or-raise-capital-with-nzte)).
- Partial NZ directories exist — **What Founders Want** ([whatfounderswant.com](https://whatfounderswant.com/))
  and **MoneyHub's** angel guide ([moneyhub.co.nz](https://www.moneyhub.co.nz/how-to-raise-money-from-angel-investors.html)) —
  but none are maintained, machine-readable, stage/region-matchable datasets.
- Overseas marketplaces (**Metal** ([metal.so](https://www.metal.so/)), **Cherub**
  ([investwithcherub.com](https://www.investwithcherub.com/))) do open investor
  matching well — for the US market.

## Questions this raises

- How many NZ startups seek angel/seed funding each year, how many fail to raise,
  and what proportion cite discovery/access (vs capital scarcity) as the barrier?
- What are the real application-to-funding conversion rates through the visible
  pathways (accelerators, angel networks, pitch events), and what happens to the
  founders who aren't selected?
- Who is actually writing the $10k–$200k cheques in NZ (individual angels outside
  formal networks?), and what would it take to surface them in an open marketplace?
- What does free / low-cost founder-investor connection look like elsewhere (open
  demo days, office hours, matching platforms), and what has evidence of working?
- Would NZ angels participate in open networking formats (all founders + all
  investors in one room) rather than curated top-5 pitch nights — and what do
  investors say stops them?
- What funding pathways exist for founders under the $250k mark who can't pay for
  conferences/cohorts, and how discoverable are they?
- Would NZ funders (angel networks, funds, government) contribute to / maintain an
  open funder directory, and what would keep it fresh?
