## Domain

`grant-access` *(closest fit — the problem is discovery/access of funding, for startups rather than charities)*

## The problem

Small, early-stage New Zealand startups struggle to find angel and seed funding — not
only because capital is scarce, but because **visibility and access are broken**.
Later-stage capital is comparatively well organised (Icehouse Ventures, the
established VC funds, NZGCP's Elevate fund-of-funds), but a first-time founder at
idea/pre-seed stage has no single NZ-focused place to see who invests at their stage,
in their region, at their cheque size, or how to approach them. Information lives
scattered across a dozen angel-network sites, fund pages and government portals — and
the funding-discovery tools founders actually find online (Metal, Cherub, most grant
marketplaces) are built for the US or other markets, not Aotearoa.

This hits hardest for founders outside Auckland/Wellington, first-time founders
without warm networks, and Māori and Pasifika founders — the people least likely to
already know an angel.

## Why it matters (any evidence you have)

- 2025 was a **record year** for NZ startup investment ($754m, up 61%), yet only
  **47 genuinely new companies received investment** — below the historical norm of
  50+ — and 83% of capital went to expansion-stage companies. Only 21% of H1 2025
  deals went to genuinely new companies vs a ~35% historical average.
  ([Young Company Finance Autumn 2026, via b2bnews](https://b2bnews.co.nz/news/nz-startup-investment-hits-754m-but-new-company-pipeline-shrinks/);
  [NZ Herald](https://www.nzherald.co.nz/business/personal-finance/investment/start-up-company-investment-surged-to-754m-in-2025-after-deal-activity-jumped/5B4X67OA4FANLGYZIUCXHTDLGI/))
- Angel capital was nearly **flat in 2025** (+2.7% to $13.9m) while deal count jumped
  34% — the same money spread much thinner per company.
  ([Catalist NZ Angel Market Report, via b2bnews](https://b2bnews.co.nz/news/nz-startup-investment-hits-754m-but-new-company-pipeline-shrinks/))
- MBIE's May 2025 briefing on access to growth capital acknowledges "regression in
  the seed funding market due to recent economic conditions".
  ([MBIE briefing](https://www.mbie.govt.nz/dmsdocument/31135-access-growth-capital))
- The Startup Advisors Council's **Upstart Nation** report (2023) found the ecosystem
  "noisy, fragmented, and disconnected", with government support spread across
  multiple agencies "making it difficult for founders to find support".
  ([Upstart Nation, MBIE](https://www.mbie.govt.nz/assets/upstart-nation.pdf);
  [Simpson Grierson summary](https://www.simpsongrierson.com/insights-news/legal-updates/upstart-nation-building-a-sustainable-startup-ecosystem))
- Discovery is churning right now: Callaghan Innovation was **disestablished on
  1 December 2025** with grants and startup programmes moved to MBIE — founders'
  bookmarks and mental maps of "where funding lives" broke overnight.
  ([Callaghan disestablishment updates](https://www.callaghaninnovation.govt.nz/about-us/disestablishment-updates/))

## What's already being done?

- **Angel Association NZ** is the peak body for ~13 angel networks but doesn't offer
  founder-facing, machine-readable discovery ([angelassociation.co.nz](https://www.angelassociation.co.nz/)).
- **NZGCP Aspire** co-invests at seed but only alongside private investors you've
  already found ([Aspire](https://www.nzgcp.co.nz/funding/aspire-seed-fund)).
- **NZTE** offers capital-raise support and 'InvestEd' education ([NZTE](https://www.nzte.govt.nz/page/invest-or-raise-capital-with-nzte)).
- Partial NZ directories exist — **What Founders Want** (government support / program
  directories, [whatfounderswant.com](https://whatfounderswant.com/)) and **MoneyHub's**
  angel-raising guide ([moneyhub.co.nz](https://www.moneyhub.co.nz/how-to-raise-money-from-angel-investors.html)) —
  but none are maintained, machine-readable, stage/region-matchable datasets.
- Overseas marketplaces (**Metal** ([metal.so](https://www.metal.so/)), **Cherub**
  ([investwithcherub.com](https://www.investwithcherub.com/))) do investor matching
  well — for the US market.

## Questions this raises

- How many NZ startups seek angel/seed funding each year, and how many fail to raise
  — and what proportion cite discovery/access (vs capital scarcity) as the barrier?
- Where does authoritative data on NZ early-stage funders (stage, region, cheque
  size, criteria) actually live, and how much is public vs held inside networks?
- What happened to founder-facing grant discovery after Callaghan Innovation's
  disestablishment — what's actually open today and where is it published?
- Do founders outside the main centres raise at materially lower rates, and does
  proximity to an angel network predict raising success?
- What funding pathways exist specifically for Māori and Pasifika founders, and how
  discoverable are they?
- Would NZ funders (angel networks, funds, government) contribute to / maintain an
  open funder directory, and what would keep it fresh?
